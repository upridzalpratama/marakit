Marakit was previously Startiq Digital, founded in 2025. We rebranded so the name says what we do: build marketing systems.

Questions people usually ask.

Why not just use AI myself?

You can, and your team should. The hard part is not the tool. It is the workflow, the quality bar, and the rules for when to switch an ad off. That is what we build and hand over.

Won't the output feel generic?

Templates are built from your brand's own data: tone, products, and content that already works. A person reviews every piece before it goes live. See examples in Proof.

What if my team can't run it?

That is why you get written SOPs, 2 training sessions, and recordings. The ads checklist is designed for non specialist staff.

How much does it cost?

Build only starts at IDR 15,000,000, paid once. Build and run starts at IDR 12,000,000 per month for 3 months. We give you the exact figure on the call.

What is the difference between Build only and Build and run?

Build only: we build the system in 4 to 6 weeks and your team runs it. Build and run: we run it for 3 months while training your team, then hand everything over.

Is ad spend included?

No. Ad spend is paid straight from your account to Meta or TikTok. We never hold your ad money.

Is this right for my business?

It fits best for F&B, fashion, or service brands making IDR 200 million to 2 billion a month, with a marketing team of 1 to 3 people.
